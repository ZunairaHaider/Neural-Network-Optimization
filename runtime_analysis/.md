# Runtime Analysis — Pruning Computational Cost

Benchmarked on: **Dataset A, dim=1, sparsity targets 50% / 70% / 90%**  
To reproduce: `python runtime_analysis/benchmark.py`  
To regenerate summary: `python runtime_analysis/runtime_summary.py`

## Computational Cost Ranking (cheapest → most expensive)

| Rank | Method | Avg Runtime | Cost Tier | Scoring Mechanism |
|------|--------|------------|-----------|-------------------|
| 1 | Taylor FO | 7.96s | 🟢 Moderate | Gradient × activation (one backward pass) |
| 2 | Magnitude | 8.20s | 🟢 Moderate | Sort \|w\| — no forward pass needed |
| 3 | OBD | 10.11s | 🟡 Expensive | Diagonal Hessian (double backprop) |
| 4 | Redundancy | 11.20s | 🟡 Expensive | Pairwise neuron correlation matrix |
| 5 | **Struct** | **11.31s** | 🟡 Expensive | L2-norm of outgoing weight rows |
| 6 | **Activ** | **13.47s** | 🟡 Expensive | One forward pass on calibration data |
| 7 | SNIP | 14.07s | 🟡 Expensive | Gradient of connection sensitivity at init |
| 8 | IMP+Rewind | 14.91s | 🟡 Expensive | Repeated full retraining from init |
| 9 | OBS | 20.20s | 🔴 Very expensive | Full inverse Hessian (O(n²) per weight) |

> **Struct** and **Activ** are the two methods implemented in this branch.  
> Relative cost = avg total time / cheapest method (Taylor FO at 7.96s).

---

## Key Findings

**1. Fine-tuning dominates runtime for all methods.**  
Scoring is fast (< 0.5s) for most methods. The real cost is retraining the surviving weights.

**2. OBS is most expensive but best quality on clean data.**  
At 20.2s per target and MSE ratio of 0.97× (below baseline), it achieves the best accuracy — but at 2.5× the cost of Taylor FO.

**3. IMP+Rewind and SNIP are expensive AND poor quality on clean data.**  
Both cost ~14s per target but have the worst MSE ratios (5-7×). Not worth the cost on Dataset A.

**4. Struct has the fastest scoring time of all methods (0.012s).**  
L2-norm computation is nearly instant. Its total cost comes from fine-tuning, not scoring.

**5. Redundancy gives the best quality-per-second tradeoff.**  
At 11.2s and MSE ratio 1.24×, it stays under the 1.3× threshold at moderate cost.
